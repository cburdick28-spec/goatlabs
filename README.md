# Prime Reel

Spin a reel of real pros, draft each one into an attribute slot on your own custom athlete, then sim the whole career to a Hall of Fame verdict.

- 11 sports: pitcher, hitter, basketball, quarterback, soccer, hockey, golf, tennis, UFC, Formula 1, cricket
- About 290 real players with estimated prime ratings
- Free play and a daily challenge (same reel order for everyone, seeded by date)
- Face a rival after any career and compare Hall of Fame scores
- 11 achievements, a daily streak and a Hall of Fame, all saved in the browser (localStorage)
- Single static file, no build step: open `index.html`

Independent fan project with no affiliation to any league, team or player. Ratings are rough estimates, not official stats.
